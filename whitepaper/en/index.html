Public research overview

This page summarizes positions, research questions, and public work already established in the public repositories. Unpublished research methods, system structure, and implementation details are outside this page’s scope.

Core position

MirrorTone / MTEL studies a question that becomes more important as AI capability grows: capability is not authority. Ability does not itself confer permission, retrieval does not itself establish current state, and tool success does not itself establish completion.

MTEL Public Governance Surface

  • Capability — What can the system do?
  • Authority — Who authorizes it to act?
  • Evidence — What supports the state, decision, or claim?
  • Rollback — How are error and propagated consequences repaired?
  • Completion — Who is qualified to decide that the task is complete?

These five questions are a public governance surface; they do not define the whole of MirrorTone.

Public scope

At this stage, the public surface centers on the two public repositories, inspectable research materials, the Frontier Signals observatory, and the Lab’s public research directions. Unpublished research methods, system structure, and implementation are not described here.

This overview is not a product whitepaper, not the full MirrorTone specification, and does not establish a repository-wide license.
