AI Philosophy Clean-Room

An independent case studying whether substantive AI authorship can remain distinct from human custody, prior-project knowledge, other AI author branches and independent reviewers while preserving reconstructable provenance.

Read full paperBack to research
Public status: this work began while materials were being prepared for a possible 2026 AI Philosophy Competition submission. No submission was made. This is now independent research and does not claim participation, acceptance, peer review, endorsement or official affiliation.

Research question

This clean-room research asks whether substantive philosophical content can be generated by a designated AI author while keeping human custody, other AI authors, literature scouts, referees and prior-project knowledge in clearly separated roles with reconstructable provenance.

AI Author

Theses, premises, distinctions, examples, objections, replies and conclusions originate in the designated clean-room author branch.

Independent Review

Literature collision, philosophical refereeing and contribution auditing are separated; a review artifact does not become author input merely because it is accessible.

Human Custody

The human governs topics, sources, process, files, selection and publication without silently inserting human-originated philosophical argument.

Selected research artifact

When Variation Replaces Its Target: Identity Constraints on Causal and Counterfactual Comparison

A complete analytic-philosophy paper of 5,588 words excluding bibliography. Its central requirement is contrast matching: target individuation, represented comparison and causal conclusion must line up; formal variable continuity alone cannot decide whether a comparison is within-X variation, X/no-X, or X/X* replacement.

Clean-room workflow

Generation, literature collision, philosophical refereeing and contribution auditing were deliberately assigned to separated AI sessions and roles. The original author branch performed bounded repairs after independent criticism rather than importing a replacement thesis from the reviewer.

Provenance and boundary

The research preserves prompts, outputs, revisions, model identity, session boundaries, source inputs, actor roles, branch identity, human interventions, review inputs, transfer events, selection and release decisions. Accessibility does not itself grant author-input authority.

Limitations: this is a procedural / semantic / provenance clean-room. It does not claim to clear pretrained model weights or latent knowledge, and publication does not make the work externally peer reviewed.

Relation to WorkNet

WorkNet serves only as a process-governance and provenance boundary, separating workflow control from substantive philosophical authorship; it is not a source of philosophy for the clean-room author.
